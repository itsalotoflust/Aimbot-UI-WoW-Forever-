Aimbot UI - credits

Lorti UI Classic (artwork in media/, look and feature set)
  Lorti, Chordsy, Macumba, Knall, Jozh, Xyz, Arrone, Redskin
  The logic was re-written for the Retail-style frames used by WoW Forever.

NiceDamage (fonts in media/fonts/, idea)
  Montyburns
  Fonts are the 8 fonts shipped with NiceDamage. Original readme: media/fonts/NiceDamage-readme.txt

Everything else (window, Button Sparkle, Spell Trainer, Mana Costs, Range Alert) is original to this addon.

What's Training? by Sveng (MIT license) - the Forever spell, quest spell and weapon skill data in
the Data folder is taken unchanged from that addon (see Data\LICENSE-WhatsTraining.txt).

Diminish by Wardz (MIT license) - the idea and look of the Diminish tab (DR icons with green /
yellow / red borders and a reset timer). The Aimbot UI version is new code written for Forever's
DR event and debuffs; no code from Diminish was copied. The spell list in Data\DRSpells.lua was
written for Aimbot UI.

AutoShotTimer Forever by Antigravity and Gornax_ (GPL-3.0) - the idea of the Auto Shot Timer tab
(a bar to the next Auto Shot with a wind-up marker, held while moving or casting, and a hybrid
melee mode) and how Forever reports shots, swings and swing range. The Aimbot UI version is new
code; no code, textures or files from AutoShotTimer Forever were copied.
